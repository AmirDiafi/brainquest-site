Explore a connected journey of 625 stages, find your kind of play, and revisit the challenges that deserve another go.

Normal stage-by-stage progression is free. Optional gems unlock hints, board help, assisted solutions and confirmed shortcuts into later stages. Reading the rules and ordinary retries never require a purchase.

Free play includes occasional age-appropriate ad breaks where available. Optional monthly and yearly MindCrown+ subscriptions remove automatic breaks and add a personal five-game daily path, private gameplay insights and one hint pass every 24 hours. Reward videos remain optional for eligible players. Prices and availability are shown in the app.

MindCrown+ also unlocks twelve original app-icon choices. It is optional and does not include gem packs or stage-access purchases. Gameplay insights describe your play, not IQ or medical outcomes.
