Arabic Dialects privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy describes Arabic Dialects for iOS, bundle identifier com.localarab.app. It does not describe BrainQuest. Contact the app operator at amirdiafi2@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

Offline learning and optional accounts

Lesson progress, selected dialect, saved phrases, mistakes, learning gems and settings are stored on your device. Core offline lessons do not require personal sign-in. Device backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings; recovery is not guaranteed.

Online pronunciation uses a pseudonymous Firebase guest session without asking for your name or email. Online premium features use a separate guest wallet credential stored in the iOS Keychain. Optional Sign in with Apple can protect a wallet and read account benefits. The app does not request name or email scopes during Apple sign-in. Firebase processes authentication identifiers and credentials; the app operator does not receive your Apple password.

Pronunciation, recordings and AI chat

When you request lesson pronunciation, the requested text, dialect and speaking rate are sent through our protected service to OpenAI to generate speech. Coach feedback and chat read-aloud use the device's speech service instead of paid OpenAI speech generation.

Microphone permission is requested when you record your practice. Your latest saved take is kept on your device and excluded from app-managed cloud backup. You can listen to it, replace it or delete it. Recording alone does not upload that take.

If you explicitly agree to an optional AI word check or correction, the selected audio, target phrase and dialect are sent through our service to OpenAI for transcription and text feedback. Chat sharing consent is requested before sending messages. Your message, dialect and a short recent conversation context are sent to OpenAI. Do not send sensitive personal, medical, financial or password information.

The app stores recent chat locally. Our Firebase service stores generated results and request/accounting metadata, including request identifiers, context, timestamps and hashes used to prevent repeated charges. Saved result content can be deleted from premium practice history; deletion does not remove purchase, charging or fraud-prevention records. Our application code does not persist the uploaded raw audio in the premium ledger. OpenAI and infrastructure providers may retain data under their own policies; we do not promise zero retention by these providers.

AI speech, transcripts and explanations may be inaccurate. Recognition-based checks are not a precise assessment of accent or pronunciation. AI services are optional; offline learning remains available without uploading a recording or using chat.

See OpenAI's privacy policy and OpenAI API data controls.

Premium gems and Apple purchases

Premium gems are separate from device-local learning gems. A Firebase cloud wallet stores the premium balance, reserved charges, chat allowances, account-linking identifiers, purchase delivery and reward events. We verify Apple transactions server-side and retain product and transaction identifiers to prevent duplicate credits and handle refunds. Apple processes payments; we do not receive your payment-card details.

Consumable gems are not cash and are not restored like non-consumable purchases. Losing an unprotected guest wallet credential can lose access to that balance. Optional account protection is offered before relying on recovery across devices. Sandbox/test balances are isolated from production balances.

See Apple's privacy information. Apple and Google approval, regional availability and network access may affect purchases or ad inventory.

Optional adult advertising and video rewards

The app includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform for optional rewarded videos and occasional between-screen ads. Ads require an explicit confirmation that you are 18 or older and want ads. Without that opt-in, ads are not requested. Paid ad-free benefits and unknown account-benefit status suppress ads. The app requests non-personalised ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission; non-personalised advertising can still process data.

Google and its advertising partners may process IP address, device/app information, approximate location inferred from IP, identifiers where available and permitted, diagnostics, ad interactions and reward events for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Required consent checks must complete before ads are requested. Ads remain unavailable if the privacy check fails.

You can turn optional ads off in the app's account/privacy settings. Where Google requires it, an Ad privacy choices control opens Google's privacy options. Declining personalisation does not necessarily prohibit every form of advertising; Google's consent status determines whether ads may be requested.

Premium video rewards use a random single-use ticket, Google's signed verification callback and our cloud wallet ledger. The ticket, reward transaction identifier, ad unit and timestamps are processed to prevent fraud and duplicate credits. Test videos never add production premium gems. Local learning-gem videos and premium-gem videos have separate reward rules, disclosed before viewing. A reward is not guaranteed for an incomplete ad or failed verification.

Live inventory requires Google account/app approval, store linking and published consent messages. Availability and ad fill are not guaranteed in every country. No personal sign-in is required simply to learn offline.

See Google's privacy policy, Google advertising privacy and Firebase privacy information.

Retention, service providers and security

We use Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Google Cloud functions to run authenticated services and store wallet/result records. Service providers may process network, request and diagnostic information to operate and secure these services. Data may be processed outside your country under the providers' applicable terms and safeguards.

Device data remains until you remove it, replace it, reset the relevant data or uninstall the app, subject to device/Keychain and backup behaviour. Cloud wallet, saved-result and accounting records have no automatic short deletion period in the current service. They are retained for service operation, recovery, accounting and fraud prevention, subject to deletion requests and applicable obligations. Security, financial or legal records may need to be retained after a deletion request.

Network services use HTTPS and authenticated access. No system can guarantee perfect security. This policy does not promise that third-party providers never process or retain information.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can learn offline without purchasing, enabling ads, signing in personally or using AI chat/checks. Microphone permission can be managed in iOS Settings. Delete saved takes and premium result content with the app's controls. Clear local chat from its information/settings menu. Manage Apple purchase and sign-in permissions through Apple.

For access, correction, export, account/data deletion or other privacy requests, contact amirdiafi2@gmail.com. Include the app name and a description of your request, not passwords or full payment information. We may need to verify ownership before acting on a cloud-wallet request. Device-local data cannot be remotely recovered or deleted by the operator. Deleting a wallet or its credentials can permanently remove access to consumable gems.

Depending on your location, applicable law may provide additional rights, including objection, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent and a complaint to a data-protection authority. Data independently held by Apple, Google or OpenAI is also governed by their privacy controls and policies.

Audience and updates

The native app's advertising is restricted to adults who explicitly opt in. Do not enable ads or send a child's personal information through AI or support features. Contact the operator if you believe a child has supplied personal information. The app is for language practice, not professional or high-stakes advice.

Updates to this policy appear on this page with a revised date. Significant changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. This policy describes app behaviour and does not replace Apple's, Google's or OpenAI's policies.
