Gems and purchases

Apple processes gem purchases. If a purchase was charged but gems have not arrived, keep the app installed, reconnect to the internet, and reopen the gem shop so unfinished transactions can retry. Contact support if it remains unresolved; do not purchase again just to retry delivery.

The updated gem shop includes 100, 500 and 1,000-gem packs when available from Apple. Prices are localised by the App Store for your storefront, with the final amount shown before payment. Smaller gem artwork gently shimmers when Reduce Motion is off; it does not indicate a time-limited offer or change the contents of a pack.

Consumable gems and puzzle progress currently stay on your device. There is no cross-device wallet or guaranteed recovery after deleting the app. Apple's Restore Purchases mechanism does not restore spent consumables.

For refund requests, visit Apple's Report a Problem.

In the MindCrown update, MindCrown+ is an optional monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, accessible from Home, Profile and Settings. While active it removes automatic between-game ads, adds a five-game daily personal practice path and local gameplay insights, and provides one hint pass every 24 hours. Explicitly chosen reward videos remain optional for eligible players. UK prices are £1.99 per month or £9.99 per year, charged upfront; other storefronts show Apple's local price. The yearly price is charged in full each year, not as monthly instalments. Use Restore purchases to restore an active subscription on the same Apple Account. Manage or cancel it in Apple subscription settings. Cancelling renewal normally keeps access until the paid period ends. Apple's purchase sheet shows the actual price and billing period before you confirm. The core game journey, ordinary retries and learning explanations remain free.

The daily path balances game activities, your chosen play style, unfinished puzzles, recent play and recorded moments of difficulty. It stays fixed for the day unless your preferences or available games change. Insights describe this device's recent play, not IQ, medical conditions or an innate brain type. Finish-time comparisons require at least three unassisted, hint-free finishes of the same game and difficulty; pause, background and blocked-menu time is excluded. Younger players are not given speed targets. A pass is used before charging 10 gems for a hint, returns if the hint fails, does not accumulate or convert to gems, and becomes available again 24 hours after successful use. Local path, history and pass timing are not a cross-device entitlement or wallet.

New first personal finishes earn 2 gems for Easy, 3 for Medium or 4 for Hard, using the difficulty actually played. A newly completed stage earns 5 additional gems after a personal finish, once only. Replays cannot farm rewards. Assisted solves grant no XP or gem rewards, but a later independent finish of a newly assisted puzzle can still earn its first personal reward. Existing completed saves are conservatively treated as already rewarded, with no retroactive credits or changes to saved balances. Hints cost 10 gems, full assisted solves 50, fresh random boards 30 and optional revives 10. Ordinary restart, undo, rules and learning explanations are free; failed assistance is refunded.

The gold MindCrown+ daily card has a Start or Continue button for the next unfinished game in your personal path, while completed entries remain available to replay. Choose balanced discovery or an activity focus: up to two different available focus worlds lead the five-game path, followed by variety. Changing focus explicitly refreshes the path without adding bonus rewards. Finishing all five shows a gentle completion message rather than automatically generating another path. The golden hint-pass status shows when it is ready or its next availability time. These improvements do not change subscription prices, the 24-hour pass rule or free stage progression.

Everyone can browse the app-icon gallery in Profile or Settings. The original MindCrown icon is free; an active MindCrown+ subscription unlocks twelve original brain-and-crown styles: Gold Member, IQ Puzzle, Maths Nerd, Computer Coder, Rainbow Pride, Creative Artist, Cosmic Explorer, Garden Calm, Sporty Spark, Smart Scholar, Pixel Gamer and Midnight Gold. These are cosmetic designs, not IQ scores or assessments of the person choosing them. Tapping a locked icon opens subscription plans, not an automatic purchase. The selected icon changes the real Home Screen icon, in-app branding and reminder artwork after iOS confirms the change. When Apple confirms that Plus has ended, the app returns to the free icon; progress and earned streak companions are unchanged. iOS controls the small system icon and notification layout.

Play insights separate all-time saved adventure progress, including assisted finishes, from new session history. The animated activity ring describes the activities you have explored, not ability. Seven-day and 30-day journals show daily sessions; the all-sessions view uses the retained history with a last-30-days chart. Game practice badges count distinct puzzles finished without hints or auto-solve, so repeat wins do not inflate them. Detailed game cards show saved progress, help use and the next practice milestone. Typical active times require three clean finishes in the same game and difficulty tier, are not shown to under-13 players and have no speed targets. Up to the last 1,000 attempts are retained locally; older attempts may no longer appear. These features do not measure IQ, brain dominance, innate ability or health.

Adult non-subscribers may see a dismissible No Ads invitation after three successfully presented and closed automatic ad breaks, at most once in seven days. It does not appear after optional reward videos, failed ads or unavailable ads. Viewing plans does not start a subscription, and declining never blocks continued play.

Game Center and rewarded videos

Game Center shares scores and achievements with Apple, not your complete puzzle save. Check your Game Center sign-in in iOS Settings if connection fails.

Video rewards are delivered only when the advertising SDK confirms completion. Closing early, unavailable ads, or failed loads do not earn rewards. You can continue playing without watching ads.

Optional reward videos are not offered to players who select Under 13. Ages 13 and above can request them with the relevant privacy protections. Prefer not to say can also request them with child-directed safeguards. Privacy permission, regional checks and available inventory still apply. The grown-up check protects purchases, not access to the gem shop's ordinary free-play information.

The MindCrown update asks for your age group and preferred play style during first-launch setup, before advertising is requested. Adults do not encounter the app's local grown-up check. Younger and unspecified-age players need it to change their saved age group, make purchases, share outside the app or open external links and sensitive privacy controls, but not simply to browse subscription plans, change play style or view reminder preferences. Apple's purchase confirmation and any Apple-managed parental approval still apply. Age-appropriate, non-personalised advertising may be available across groups only where privacy checks and inventory permit. Existing disabled advertising permission stays disabled. Earlier BrainQuest versions may still require an eligible age of 16 or older. A failed privacy check never spends gems or grants an assisted completion.

Under-13 gameplay adapts from gentle to medium puzzles, reduces moving-game difficulty and removes the Hungry Hole countdown. Failure offers a free retry rather than a purchase or video reward prompt. Home and Profile do not show subscription promotions to this group. Stage progress and game identifiers remain the same.

Verified friend invitations - in development

The developing program gives the inviter 50 gems after a new friend's first verified independent finish, up to 10 rewards per UTC calendar month. It is optional, free to join, and does not change gem-pack or subscription prices. Production rewards are not open yet. Ordinary app-link sharing works now; do not expect a reward from sharing alone or from an earlier app version.

When available, open You > Invite & share to join, then share your code using Apple's share controls. Your new friend enters the code there before their first finish and plays an independent game while online. Hints are allowed; auto-solve does not qualify. Existing players can invite but cannot become newcomers again, and self-invitations or repeat finishes cannot earn another reward. A friend finishing after the month's ten-reward limit does not carry into a later month. Invitations disclose that the inviter may earn gems.

Verification needs a connected Game Center account and a physical iPhone supporting App Attest. It verifies account signatures and app origin, not human uniqueness, legal age, IQ or mathematical proof of a puzzle solution. Games remain playable offline without enrolling. An interrupted verified finish or gem delivery retries when you return online; an expired game-verification ticket may require another independent online finish if the account remains eligible. Refresh verified rewards from Invite & share. Keep the app installed and contact support if delivery remains unresolved.

Gem delivery is deduplicated and a claimed receipt is attached to one verified installation. Game Center does not synchronize the gem wallet, and a claimed reward cannot be restored into another installation. Declared Under-13 or unspecified-age players cannot enroll. You may stop verification on this device; eligibility and receipt records remain to prevent duplicates. See the privacy policy before using verification. Development-test rewards are explicitly labelled and never change the real gem balance.

Streak companions and sharing

Finish at least one game each day to grow a consecutive-day streak. Replays and assisted finishes count once for the day, without adding extra first-win XP or gems. Companions unlock at 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 300, 500, 700 and 1,000 days, alongside your starter companion. Collected companions stay yours after a break; choose one from Home or Profile.

Open Profile's achievement studio to preview an Adventure or Streak Crew card and share a generated image with the app link and QR code. Younger and unspecified-age players encounter a grown-up check before sharing. Invitations do not earn referral gems or claim that a friend has joined; no referral-tracking service is used.

Achievement cards celebrate device-local gameplay, including assisted completions. Their activity descriptions are not IQ measurements, brain-health advice, verified qualifications or evidence of improved everyday abilities. A missing or lost local save cannot be independently certified or reconstructed from a shared image.

The starter companion has rounded artwork; selected companions gently glow, move and blink when Reduce Motion is off. In-app crowned-brain mascots can blink too; the iPhone app icon and exported images remain static. Exported cards include Apple's App Store badge and a scannable official app link.

Reminders and celebrations

Open Settings > Streak reminders, or use the reminder entry in your companion collection. Reminders are optional, with a 7:00 PM local default and your own time picker. Ordinary reminder controls do not require a grown-up check; opening iOS Settings still does for younger and unspecified-age players. For protected actions, the local attention check asks for three specified letters across two words, entered in reverse order. It has no maths or timer, collects no answer or identity, and is not verified parental consent, reliable proof of age or a certified robot-detection service.

Allow iOS notifications when you choose to enable reminders. If permission was denied, enable it in the app's iOS Settings and return. Each reminder has one short title and subtitle, with rotating game-topic artwork, your selected app icon and collected companion. A saved activity focus receives extra turns, mixed with other worlds. Tap to browse the pictured world; no game starts automatically. iOS decides how much appears in a banner, the app-icon size and whether Focus or Scheduled Summary delays delivery. Sound follows Game sounds and system settings.

Finish a game to skip that day's remaining reminder. Changing the time after a reminder was already due does not create another one that day. We schedule up to two weeks and refresh the plan when you return or play; after two quiet weeks, reminders pause. Turning them off cancels the app's queued streak reminders. Your collected companions are never removed for missing a day.

A verified gem delivery or new active subscription can show a brief celebration with light sound and haptics, following your preferences. Cancelling, pending approval, recurring renewals and repeated transaction callbacks do not create a new purchase celebration. A celebration is not a second payment or an undisclosed extra gem award. The updated first-finish and stage gem rules are described above; existing balances and XP are preserved.
