A little help.

Contact support

Email amirdiafi2@gmail.com for app support or privacy questions. Include your iOS version, app version, game number, and what happened. Do not send passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive information.

Gems and purchases

Apple processes gem purchases. If a purchase was charged but gems have not arrived, keep the app installed, reconnect to the internet, and reopen the gem shop so unfinished transactions can retry. Contact support if it remains unresolved; do not purchase again just to retry delivery.

Consumable gems and puzzle progress currently stay on your device. There is no cross-device wallet or guaranteed recovery after deleting the app. Apple's Restore Purchases mechanism does not restore spent consumables.

For refund requests, visit Apple's Report a Problem.

Game Center and rewarded videos

Game Center shares scores and achievements with Apple, not your complete puzzle save. Check your Game Center sign-in in iOS Settings if connection fails.

Video rewards are delivered only when the advertising SDK confirms completion. Closing early, unavailable ads, or failed loads do not earn rewards. You can continue playing without watching ads.
