A little help.

Contact support

Email amirdiafi2@gmail.com for app support or privacy questions. Include your iOS version, app version, game number, and what happened. Do not send passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive information.

Gems and purchases

Apple processes gem purchases. If a purchase was charged but gems have not arrived, keep the app installed, reconnect to the internet, and reopen the gem shop so unfinished transactions can retry. Contact support if it remains unresolved; do not purchase again just to retry delivery.

The updated gem shop includes 100, 500 and 1,000-gem packs when available from Apple. Prices are localised by the App Store for your storefront, with the final amount shown before payment. Smaller gem artwork gently shimmers when Reduce Motion is off; it does not indicate a time-limited offer or change the contents of a pack.

Consumable gems and puzzle progress currently stay on your device. There is no cross-device wallet or guaranteed recovery after deleting the app. Apple's Restore Purchases mechanism does not restore spent consumables.

For refund requests, visit Apple's Report a Problem.

In the MindCrown update, No ad breaks is an optional monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription. It removes automatic between-game ads while active; explicitly chosen videos to earn gems remain available. Use Restore purchases in the subscription screen to restore an active subscription on the same Apple Account. Manage or cancel it in Apple subscription settings. Cancelling renewal normally keeps access until the paid period ends. Apple's purchase sheet shows the actual price and billing period before you confirm.

Game Center and rewarded videos

Game Center shares scores and achievements with Apple, not your complete puzzle save. Check your Game Center sign-in in iOS Settings if connection fails.

Video rewards are delivered only when the advertising SDK confirms completion. Closing early, unavailable ads, or failed loads do not earn rewards. You can continue playing without watching ads.

Optional reward videos are not offered to players who select Under 13. Ages 13 and above can request them with the relevant privacy protections. Prefer not to say can also request them with child-directed safeguards. Privacy permission, regional checks and available inventory still apply. The grown-up check protects purchases, not access to the gem shop's ordinary free-play information.

The MindCrown update asks for your age group and preferred play style during first-launch setup, before advertising is requested. Change them in Settings; younger and unspecified-age players use a grown-up area for sensitive controls. Age-appropriate, non-personalised advertising may be available across groups only where privacy checks and inventory permit. Existing disabled advertising permission stays disabled. Earlier BrainQuest versions may still require an eligible age of 16 or older. A failed privacy check never spends gems or grants an assisted completion.

Streak companions and sharing

Finish at least one game each day to grow a consecutive-day streak. Replays and assisted finishes count once for the day, without adding extra first-win XP or gems. Companions unlock at 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 300, 500, 700 and 1,000 days, alongside your starter companion. Collected companions stay yours after a break; choose one from Home or Profile.

Open Profile's achievement studio to preview an Adventure or Streak Crew card and share a generated image with the app link and QR code. Younger and unspecified-age players encounter a grown-up check before sharing. Invitations do not earn referral gems or claim that a friend has joined; no referral-tracking service is used.

Achievement cards celebrate device-local gameplay, including assisted completions. Their activity descriptions are not IQ measurements, brain-health advice, verified qualifications or evidence of improved everyday abilities. A missing or lost local save cannot be independently certified or reconstructed from a shared image.

The starter companion has rounded artwork; selected companions gently glow, move and blink when Reduce Motion is off. In-app crowned-brain mascots can blink too; the iPhone app icon and exported images remain static. Exported cards include Apple's App Store badge and a scannable official app link.

Reminders and celebrations

Open Settings > Streak reminders, or use the reminder entry in your companion collection. Reminders are optional, with a 7:00 PM local default and your own time picker. Younger and unspecified-age players use the grown-up area for these controls. The local attention check asks for two specified letters from a word, in order, instead of copying the word or doing calculations. It has no timer, collects no answer or identity, and is not verified parental consent or a certified robot-detection service.

Allow iOS notifications when you choose to enable reminders. If permission was denied, enable it in the app's iOS Settings and return. A reminder includes your companion picture, a short heading and a few supporting words. iOS decides how much appears in a banner, the app-icon size and whether Focus or Scheduled Summary delays delivery. Sound follows Game sounds and system settings.

Finish a game to skip that day's remaining reminder. Changing the time after a reminder was already due does not create another one that day. We schedule up to two weeks and refresh the plan when you return or play; after two quiet weeks, reminders pause. Turning them off cancels the app's queued streak reminders. Your collected companions are never removed for missing a day.

A verified gem delivery or new active subscription can show a brief celebration with light sound and haptics, following your preferences. Cancelling, pending approval, recurring renewals and repeated transaction callbacks do not create a new purchase celebration. A celebration is not a second payment or an extra gem award. First-win XP and gem rules are unchanged.
