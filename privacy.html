Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy describes MindCrown: Fun Brain Training, previously BrainQuest: Little Brain Wins, for iOS. The new onboarding and subscription features described below are part of the MindCrown update; availability depends on your installed version and Apple's approval. Contact the app operator at amirdiafi2@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Your game data

Puzzle progress, settings, gem balances, purchased hint unlocks, and transaction identifiers used to prevent duplicate gem delivery are saved locally on your device. BrainQuest does not require a separate game account and does not currently operate a cloud wallet or developer-run analytics service.

For the optional MindCrown+ practice features, the updated app stores up to 1,000 recent local play-session records: a random record identifier, game and puzzle identifiers, applied difficulty, date, active play duration, finish status and assistance-related counters. Local history can begin before subscribing. It also stores the current daily path and the most recent hint-pass use time when those features are used. These features do not upload gameplay history, use a machine-learning service or infer IQ, medical status or an innate brain type. Insights use recent gameplay and require at least three clean finishes of the same game and difficulty before showing a typical finish time. Speed comparisons are not shown to Under-13 players.

Deleting the app can remove local progress and gems. Device backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings; recovery is not guaranteed.

First-launch onboarding asks for an age group and a preferred style of play. Only the chosen group and preference are saved on your device, not a birth date or your answers to a parent-area check. They help select game suggestions, adapt Under-13 play to gentler puzzles and calmer moving-game tiers, and apply age-appropriate advertising protections. All game worlds remain available through normal stage progression; recommendations do not diagnose or measure intelligence or development. You can update preferences in Settings. Existing players are asked to confirm the new setup without resetting progress.

Daily play streaks, the highest recorded streak and your selected collectible companion are also stored locally. Completing a game on a calendar day maintains the daily habit; repeated games on that day do not add extra streak days. Companions remain collected after a break. Existing saved streaks are preserved, but previously unrecorded historical bests cannot be reconstructed.

Optional streak reminders are local notifications, off until you choose to enable them and allow iOS notification permission. The default time is 7:00 PM in your local time; your chosen time, reminder preference and recent scheduling dates are stored on your device. Short titles, subtitles and game-topic pictures rotate using the local calendar and, when selected, your saved activity focus. Pictures include your selected app icon and collected companion. No push server, contacts or advertising identifiers are used to create them, and the copy makes no medical or intelligence claim. The app plans up to fourteen days, refreshes the schedule when you return or play, and skips reminders for days already played. Tapping a reminder opens its world without starting a game. iOS controls delivery, previews, sound and icon sizes; Focus or Scheduled Summary can delay them. You can turn reminders off in the app or iOS Settings.

Your app-icon choice is managed by iOS; MindCrown does not upload it. Everyone can preview the gallery; extra icons require an active MindCrown+ subscription. After Apple confirms that a subscription has ended, the app returns to the free icon without deleting progress or earned companions.

Invitations and achievement cards

The achievement studio generates shareable PNG images on your device using recorded game completions, XP, streaks and collected companions. Cards include the generation date, an official App Store link and a QR code. They do not include your chosen age group, Game Center name or contact details. Counts include assisted completions and are personal device-local records, not independently verified credentials.

Cards describe the kinds of activities used in games. They are not IQ tests, brain-health assessments, diagnoses, certificates of intelligence, or proof of benefits in everyday life. No gameplay record is sent to an image-generation service to create a personal card.

Invitations and exports open Apple's sharing controls. The app does not read your contacts or automatically send messages. You choose the destination and whether to complete a share. A local grown-up gate precedes sharing controls for younger or unspecified-age players. Anything you choose to share is then subject to the receiving app or service's privacy policies.

Optional verified invitations

Verified invitation rewards are not yet open in the publicly available app. The developing feature reuses Apple Game Center and App Attest rather than asking for a new password or accessing contacts. Ordinary games, local insights and sharing the official app link do not require joining it. Declared Under-13 and unspecified-age groups cannot enroll; younger eligible players use the existing local grown-up protection before accessing external sharing. This local check is not identity verification or verified legal parental consent.

The upcoming program issues separate 100-gem receipts to an eligible inviter and new friend after the friend's first verified independent full-app finish. The inviter's ten-per-UTC-month cap does not remove the friend's once-per-account welcome bonus. Eligibility uses recorded program history and the official client's declaration; it does not determine an Apple Account's creation date or human uniqueness. Older receipts retain their original amount. A shared website invitation URL contains the invitation code, not an account identifier or identity proof. Opening the page or installed-app link does not enroll a player or award gems. The page uses no analytics, cookies or automatic redirect; GitHub Pages processes ordinary website request metadata under GitHub's privacy policy.

Choosing a verification or enrollment button authorizes the app to send a team-scoped Game Center player identifier and Apple's signed identity proof, along with app-verification material, to the MindCrown verifier hosted by Cloudflare. The identifier is processed to check Apple's signature; the service then retains an app-scoped hash, not your Game Center alias or real name. App Attest verification keys and monotonic request counters, invitation associations, first-finish eligibility, a qualifying puzzle identifier, verification timestamps and reward receipt/delivery states are retained to operate the program and prevent repeated claims. No birthday, contacts, full session history or private signing key is sent. Availability checks use only normal connection metadata, not the identity-verification material.

Cloudflare processes normal network metadata, including IP addresses, to operate and protect its infrastructure. The verifier uses an IP hash for short-lived request limiting. Expired rate records are cleaned on subsequent requests after two hours, and expired nonces and game tickets are also cleaned during requests. Minimal eligibility and receipt records remain when verification is stopped on a device, so reinstalling cannot create a second newcomer reward. These records are used for invitation verification and delivery, not advertising profiles, IQ assessment or a cross-device gem wallet.

You can stop new verification on your device from Invite & share. Previously shared codes and eligibility/receipt records may remain; contact support for access, correction, deletion or other requests regarding these records. Requests are handled subject to applicable obligations and legitimate fraud-prevention needs. Do not delete the app as a way to retry a missing reward: a claimed receipt belongs to its verified installation and cannot be credited again on another device. Apple and Cloudflare may process data internationally under their applicable policies and safeguards. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and Apple's privacy information.

Apple purchases and Game Center

Apple processes payments. BrainQuest receives verified transaction information needed to deliver gems, such as the product and transaction identifiers. The app does not receive your payment-card details.

Purchase celebrations are shown only after verified gem delivery or an active purchased subscription, not cancelled or pending payments. Device-local transaction identifiers prevent duplicate celebrations as well as duplicate gem delivery. Celebrations do not add undisclosed bonus gems, change subscription terms, or use a separate profiling or analytics service. Their light sounds follow the game sound setting and iOS Silent Mode, and motion follows Reduce Motion.

Optional monthly and yearly MindCrown+ subscriptions remove automatic between-game advertising and enable the personal daily practice path, local insights and one hint pass every 24 hours while active. Passes do not accumulate or convert to gems; pass cooldown, practice history and progress stay local to this device. Explicitly chosen reward videos remain available to eligible subscribers. Prices and billing periods are displayed from the App Store before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple; manage or cancel them in your Apple Account subscription settings. Restore purchases checks active subscription entitlements but does not reconstruct deleted gameplay history. Consumable gem balances are local and are not restored as a cross-device wallet. Apple's standard end-user licence agreement applies.

If you use Game Center, Apple handles authentication, leaderboards, and achievements. BrainQuest accesses your Game Center display name and authentication state and submits game scores and achievement progress. Your complete puzzle save is not uploaded to Game Center by this app.

See Apple's privacy information for Apple's handling of this data.

Advertising and video rewards

MindCrown includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK for optional rewarded videos and occasional advertising between completed games. Ads are never a requirement to start ordinary play and are not requested before the new player setup is complete. Between-game ads follow a cold-start delay and frequency limits, and use the advertising provider's close or skip controls. App Store builds use production advertising placements; development builds use Google's sample inventory. Test inventory can still involve data processing.

The upcoming rewarded-video policy stores shown-video timestamps and the last dismissal time locally to enforce a 90-second cooldown and fifteen shown videos per rolling 24 hours across gem and assistance rewards. It also counts at most five shown videos in each app session. This pacing history is not uploaded to the developer or used for personalisation. An impression counts even if no reward is earned; failed loads do not count. Provider processing of ad impressions and rewards remains separate.

When advertising services are used, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, diagnostics, ad interactions, and reward events. This supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation. BrainQuest currently requests non-personalised ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalised advertising can still involve data processing.

In the MindCrown update, occasional advertising may be available across the selected age groups, subject to legal requirements, Google's privacy checks and appropriate inventory. Under-13 users and users who prefer not to state an age receive Google's child-directed treatment. Teen groups receive Google's under-age-of-consent treatment. All requests use Google's general-audience content limit and non-personalised treatment; publisher first-party identifiers are disabled. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency or location permission. These SDK settings are safeguards, not a guarantee of legal eligibility in every region. If required privacy checks do not permit advertising, no ads are requested. Earlier BrainQuest releases may continue to block advertising for under-16 or unspecified ages.

The updated app does not offer optional rewarded videos to players who select Under 13. Players who select 13-15, 16-17, 18 or older, or Prefer not to say can request optional videos when advertising permission and Google's privacy checks permit. Prefer not to say retains child-directed safeguards; it is not treated as an adult declaration. This reward-video age rule is an app design choice, not a statement that Apple bans every ad below a universal age threshold. Automatic breaks remain separately subject to privacy permission, legal eligibility, frequency limits, subscription status and suitable inventory; they are never guaranteed across all users or regions.

For eligible users with videos enabled, Google's User Messaging Platform refreshes privacy information each app launch. Any required Google consent form must complete before an ad is requested. Failed privacy checks block videos, not gameplay. Google determines whether ads may be requested after your choices; declining personalisation does not necessarily mean that every form of advertising is prohibited. BrainQuest continues to request non-personalised ads.

Use the privacy and grown-up area in Settings to review advertising permissions and Google's available privacy choices. Existing disabled advertising permissions are not silently enabled by the update. A No ad breaks subscription removes automatic breaks but does not make an explicitly requested reward video mandatory or remove its third-party processing. A local grown-up challenge places friction before purchases, external links and age-preference editing for younger or unspecified-age players; it is not identity verification or verified parental consent. Apple's purchase approval controls also apply.

Ad availability depends on Google's app readiness checks, regional consent, and available inventory. Production placement configuration does not guarantee that a video will be available. No reward is granted unless the advertising provider confirms completion. This policy will be updated if the release implementation changes.

Learn more at Google advertising privacy and Google's privacy policy. Third-party processing, international transfers, and retention are governed by those providers' policies and applicable law.

Support communications

If you email support, the app operator receives your email address and any information you choose to provide. This is used to respond, investigate issues, and handle privacy requests, and is retained only as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Do not send sensitive medical, financial, or password information.

Your choices and requests

You can play without buying gems or choosing reward videos. Free play may contain occasional between-game advertising where permitted. Use the in-app privacy controls to manage permissions and Google's available choices, and Apple's subscription controls to manage No ad breaks. Manage Game Center through iOS Settings and Apple purchase requests through Apple. Contact the email above to request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence, or to raise other privacy concerns. Depending on your location, applicable law may provide additional rights, including objection, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent, or a complaint to a data-protection authority.

The app operator cannot access or remotely delete a device-local save. Deleting the app can delete that save and consumable gems. Data held independently by Apple or Google must be managed through their own privacy controls.

Audience and educational content

MindCrown is a general-audience puzzle collection with age-appropriate recommendations and protective advertising settings. No exact birthday or separate game account is requested. Younger users should involve a parent or guardian in purchases and privacy decisions. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support or advertising should not have been requested, contact the email above.

Knowledge challenges, including medical topics, are for general education and entertainment, not medical advice.
