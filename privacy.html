Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy describes MindCrown: Fun Brain Training, previously BrainQuest: Little Brain Wins, for iOS. The new onboarding and subscription features described below are part of the MindCrown update; availability depends on your installed version and Apple's approval. Contact the app operator at amirdiafi2@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Your game data

Puzzle progress, settings, gem balances, purchased hint unlocks, and transaction identifiers used to prevent duplicate gem delivery are saved locally on your device. BrainQuest does not require a separate game account and does not currently operate a cloud wallet or developer-run analytics service.

Deleting the app can remove local progress and gems. Device backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings; recovery is not guaranteed.

First-launch onboarding asks for an age group and a preferred style of play. Only the chosen group and preference are saved on your device, not a birth date or your answers to a parent-area check. They help select game suggestions and apply age-appropriate advertising protections. All game worlds remain available; recommendations do not diagnose or measure intelligence or development. You can update preferences in Settings. Existing players are asked to confirm the new setup without resetting progress.

Apple purchases and Game Center

Apple processes payments. BrainQuest receives verified transaction information needed to deliver gems, such as the product and transaction identifiers. The app does not receive your payment-card details.

Optional monthly and yearly No ad breaks subscriptions remove automatic between-game advertising while the subscription is active. Explicitly chosen reward videos remain available, including to subscribers who want to earn gems. Prices and billing periods are displayed from the App Store before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple; manage or cancel them in your Apple Account subscription settings. Restore purchases checks active subscription entitlements. Consumable gem balances are local and are not restored as a cross-device wallet. Apple's standard end-user licence agreement applies.

If you use Game Center, Apple handles authentication, leaderboards, and achievements. BrainQuest accesses your Game Center display name and authentication state and submits game scores and achievement progress. Your complete puzzle save is not uploaded to Game Center by this app.

See Apple's privacy information for Apple's handling of this data.

Advertising and video rewards

MindCrown includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK for optional rewarded videos and occasional advertising between completed games. Ads are never a requirement to start ordinary play and are not requested before the new player setup is complete. Between-game ads follow a cold-start delay and frequency limits, and use the advertising provider's close or skip controls. App Store builds use production advertising placements; development builds use Google's sample inventory. Test inventory can still involve data processing.

When advertising services are used, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, diagnostics, ad interactions, and reward events. This supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation. BrainQuest currently requests non-personalised ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalised advertising can still involve data processing.

In the MindCrown update, occasional advertising may be available across the selected age groups, subject to legal requirements, Google's privacy checks and appropriate inventory. Under-13 users and users who prefer not to state an age receive Google's child-directed treatment. Teen groups receive Google's under-age-of-consent treatment. All requests use Google's general-audience content limit and non-personalised treatment; publisher first-party identifiers are disabled. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency or location permission. These SDK settings are safeguards, not a guarantee of legal eligibility in every region. If required privacy checks do not permit advertising, no ads are requested. Earlier BrainQuest releases may continue to block advertising for under-16 or unspecified ages.

For eligible users with videos enabled, Google's User Messaging Platform refreshes privacy information each app launch. Any required Google consent form must complete before an ad is requested. Failed privacy checks block videos, not gameplay. Google determines whether ads may be requested after your choices; declining personalisation does not necessarily mean that every form of advertising is prohibited. BrainQuest continues to request non-personalised ads.

Use the privacy and grown-up area in Settings to review advertising permissions and Google's available privacy choices. Existing disabled advertising permissions are not silently enabled by the update. A No ad breaks subscription removes automatic breaks but does not make an explicitly requested reward video mandatory or remove its third-party processing. A local grown-up challenge places friction before purchases, external links and age-preference editing for younger or unspecified-age players; it is not identity verification or verified parental consent. Apple's purchase approval controls also apply.

Ad availability depends on Google's app readiness checks, regional consent, and available inventory. Production placement configuration does not guarantee that a video will be available. No reward is granted unless the advertising provider confirms completion. This policy will be updated if the release implementation changes.

Learn more at Google advertising privacy and Google's privacy policy. Third-party processing, international transfers, and retention are governed by those providers' policies and applicable law.

Support communications

If you email support, the app operator receives your email address and any information you choose to provide. This is used to respond, investigate issues, and handle privacy requests, and is retained only as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Do not send sensitive medical, financial, or password information.

Your choices and requests

You can play without buying gems or choosing reward videos. Free play may contain occasional between-game advertising where permitted. Use the in-app privacy controls to manage permissions and Google's available choices, and Apple's subscription controls to manage No ad breaks. Manage Game Center through iOS Settings and Apple purchase requests through Apple. Contact the email above to request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence, or to raise other privacy concerns. Depending on your location, applicable law may provide additional rights, including objection, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent, or a complaint to a data-protection authority.

The app operator cannot access or remotely delete a device-local save. Deleting the app can delete that save and consumable gems. Data held independently by Apple or Google must be managed through their own privacy controls.

Audience and educational content

MindCrown is a general-audience puzzle collection with age-appropriate recommendations and protective advertising settings. No exact birthday or separate game account is requested. Younger users should involve a parent or guardian in purchases and privacy decisions. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support or advertising should not have been requested, contact the email above.

Knowledge challenges, including medical topics, are for general education and entertainment, not medical advice.

Policy changes

Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Significant changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. This policy describes app behaviour; it does not replace Apple's or Google's policies.
