Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy describes BrainQuest: Little Brain Wins for iOS. Contact the app operator at amirdiafi2@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Your game data

Puzzle progress, settings, gem balances, purchased hint unlocks, and transaction identifiers used to prevent duplicate gem delivery are saved locally on your device. BrainQuest does not require a separate game account and does not currently operate a cloud wallet or developer-run analytics service.

Deleting the app can remove local progress and gems. Device backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings; recovery is not guaranteed.

Apple purchases and Game Center

Apple processes payments. BrainQuest receives verified transaction information needed to deliver gems, such as the product and transaction identifiers. The app does not receive your payment-card details.

If you use Game Center, Apple handles authentication, leaderboards, and achievements. BrainQuest accesses your Game Center display name and authentication state and submits game scores and achievement progress. Your complete puzzle save is not uploaded to Game Center by this app.

See Apple's privacy information for Apple's handling of this data.

Advertising and video rewards

BrainQuest includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK for optional rewarded videos and occasional between-game video breaks with advance notice and a Skip option. Release builds use production advertising placements; development builds use Google's sample inventory to avoid invalid live-ad traffic. Either type can involve data processing.

When advertising services are used, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, diagnostics, ad interactions, and reward events. This supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation. BrainQuest currently requests non-personalised ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalised advertising can still involve data processing.

Advertising privacy controls are available in Settings, the gem shop, and the assisted-solve screen. Advertising videos are available only after choosing an age group of 16 or older. Users under 16, users who prefer not to state their age group, and users who have not chosen an age group cannot request videos or receive automatic video breaks. Only the selected age group is stored locally, not a birth date.

For eligible users with videos enabled, Google's User Messaging Platform refreshes privacy information each app launch. Any required Google consent form must complete before an ad is requested. Failed privacy checks block videos, not gameplay. Google determines whether ads may be requested after your choices; declining personalisation does not necessarily mean that every form of advertising is prohibited. BrainQuest continues to request non-personalised ads.

You can turn off advertising videos at any time using Allow advertising videos. Use Manage advertising privacy choices (or Check advertising privacy choices) to open the privacy options required by Google. Eligible users can still manage existing choices after turning videos off. Teen ad requests use Google's teenage treatment, all requests limit ad content to Google's general-audience rating, and publisher first-party identifiers and SDK coarse-location collection are disabled. The app does not request location permission.

Ad availability depends on Google's app readiness checks, regional consent, and available inventory. Production placement configuration does not guarantee that a video will be available. No reward is granted unless the advertising provider confirms completion. This policy will be updated if the release implementation changes.

Learn more at Google advertising privacy and Google's privacy policy. Third-party processing, international transfers, and retention are governed by those providers' policies and applicable law.

Support communications

If you email support, the app operator receives your email address and any information you choose to provide. This is used to respond, investigate issues, and handle privacy requests, and is retained only as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Do not send sensitive medical, financial, or password information.

Your choices and requests

You can play without buying gems or watching videos. Use the in-app advertising privacy controls to turn videos off or manage Google's available choices. Manage Game Center through iOS Settings and Apple purchase requests through Apple. Contact the email above to request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence, or to raise other privacy concerns. Depending on your location, applicable law may provide additional rights, including objection, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent, or a complaint to a data-protection authority.

The app operator cannot access or remotely delete a device-local save. Deleting the app can delete that save and consumable gems. Data held independently by Apple or Google must be managed through their own privacy controls.

Audience and educational content

BrainQuest is intended for a general audience including teenagers, not specifically directed to children under 13. Advertising videos are blocked for anyone under 16 or without a confirmed eligible age group. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact the email above.

Knowledge challenges, including medical topics, are for general education and entertainment, not medical advice.

Policy changes

Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Significant changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. This policy describes app behaviour; it does not replace Apple's or Google's policies.
